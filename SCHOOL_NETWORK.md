# Getting the domain unblocked on school networks

The app domain may be blocked by school content filters (GoGuardian, Lightspeed, Securly, Cisco Umbrella, etc.) because new/uncategorized domains are blocked by default.

## Steps to request unblocking

1. **Identify the filter vendor** — ask the school IT admin which content filtering product they use.
2. **Submit a recategorization request:**
   - GoGuardian: https://www.goguardian.com/contact
   - Lightspeed: https://www.lightspeedsystems.com/support/
   - Securly: https://www.securly.com/contact-us
   - Cisco Umbrella: https://support.umbrella.com/hc/en-us/requests/new
   - Generic: Most vendors have a "request review" or "misclassified site" form — search "[vendor name] site review request"
3. **Request the category:** "Education / Educational Technology"
4. **IT admin override (fastest path):** Ask the school IT admin to manually allowlist the domain in their filter console. This takes minutes and doesn't require waiting for vendor review.
5. **Interim option:** Have your teacher pilot from a personal device on a personal network (home wifi, mobile hotspot) — school filters only apply on school network/devices.
