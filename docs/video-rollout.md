# Teacher video rollout

Video is included in the existing $20/month Teacher subscription. The plan keeps its existing 300 AI-assisted recordings per billing period. A Teacher account can authorize up to 200 video uploads per verified Stripe period; each upload is limited to 20 MiB and the assignment's 10–300 second recording limit. Audio-only assignments and Open Mic are unchanged. Video responses may be optional or required per assignment. A teacher can opt into automatic **speech-based** AI draft grading of video responses; the video itself is for human review and visual criteria must be graded by the teacher. Each delivered AI draft consumes one existing AI-assisted recording unit. There are no overages or token purchases.

## Activation

Video defaults off. Before setting `VIDEO_FEATURE_ENABLED=true`, `VIDEO_STUDENT_DATA_APPROVED=school-and-provider-reviewed-v1`, and adding a teacher to `VIDEO_APPROVED_TEACHER_EMAILS`, document the school's authority for recording students, parent notice/consent where required, the FERPA school-official agreement and controls, state and district recording rules, accessibility accommodations, retention period, incident process, and vendor DPA. Set `VIDEO_RETENTION_DAYS` to the school's approved schedule (1–90 days). Confirm the Vercel Blob store is private and the cleanup cron is running. The app enforces an active, current Stripe Teacher period for assignment setup and upload authorization.

Automatic AI drafts need separate approval: `VIDEO_AI_ENABLED=true` and `VIDEO_AI_STUDENT_DATA_APPROVED=school-and-provider-reviewed-v1`, alongside existing `AI_GRADING_ENABLED` and `AI_STUDENT_DATA_APPROVED`. For children under 13 or the applicable digital-consent age, verify an approved OpenAI Zero Data Retention configuration before sending any audio. Review the exact provider/model list and school agreement. The server extracts the submitted video's audio. The configured transcription provider receives that audio; production defaults to OpenAI `gpt-4o-transcribe` through Vercel AI Gateway unless Gateway is disabled. The grading model receives transcript text plus the assignment and rubric, with no audio or video recording. No self-hosted transcription is implemented. Video-derived submissions cannot use the shared direct-audio Gemini grading route, including after video deletion; their processing identity is separated from direct-audio reviews. It saves a draft suggestion for teacher review and does not set the final grade. Disable `autoGradeVideo` on an assignment to stop new jobs; already-started provider requests may finish.

## Storage and costs

The initial limit uses a small observed sample of existing teacher usage, with headroom above the busiest observed month. Identifying account details and individual billing records are intentionally excluded from this public repository. Historical durations are incomplete, so this sample does not forecast video adoption. Video has a 90-day default storage period and authenticated same-origin streaming playback. Upload authorization is issued before transfer; the server verifies bytes, MIME type, tracks, and actual duration before creating a submission. Uploads not finalized within one hour cannot become submissions, and orphan objects become eligible for daily cleanup after 24 hours; outages and backlogs can delay deletion. The billing-period cap counts reserved and completed uploads; a separate 400-authorization-attempt cap includes cancelled reservations. Stable upload IDs and completed-submission lookups make retries idempotent. Validation and playback each reserve at most 10 GiB and 5,000 requests per teacher per UTC calendar month. Playback reserves the whole object for every range request, so seeking and retries can exhaust the allowance earlier than actual bytes transferred would suggest.

At the published Vercel Blob rates, a full 200 × 20 MiB month writes about 3.91 GiB of video. At 90-day retention, steady storage is about 11.72 GiB, roughly **$0.27/month** per fully capped teacher at $0.023/GB-month. One server verification read plus one complete playback per video transfers about 7.82 GiB, roughly **$0.39** at $0.05/GB; verification plus ten full plays costs about **$2.15**. The actual bill also depends on operations, cache behavior, retained copies, and the provider's accounting units. Client uploads themselves do not incur Blob data-transfer charges. This estimate excludes base hosting, Stripe fees, support, taxes, and AI. These are illustrative Blob-only estimates, **not a guaranteed profit floor**. Playback now requires app authorization on every request and has server-side resource reservations. Proxy playback also incurs applicable function, request, and delivery costs; AI retry and concurrency bounds and the complete production rate model still need verification before quoting a worst-case subscription margin.

Speech transcription for 300 five-minute reviews would cost about **$9.00** with the code's production default `gpt-4o-transcribe` ($0.006/minute), or **$4.50** with `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` ($0.003/minute), before grading model tokens and retries. At the published US domestic-card rate, Stripe Payments is about **$0.88** on a $20 charge; Stripe Billing pay-as-you-go adds about **$0.14** if that fee applies. The exact production model mix, token volumes, retry rates, and payment-method fees need metering before quoting a worst-case net margin. The existing global provider budget and per-teacher limits remain in force. Automatic drafts pause when the 300-unit allowance or safety budget is exhausted; manual grading and video playback remain available.
