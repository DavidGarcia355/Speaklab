# HablaMan lab assets

`land.geojson`: Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, public domain.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The lab rasterizes this geographic outline in the browser using HablaMan's blue/green palette. No remote assets or services are requested at runtime. The production buckle PNG is reused directly as the shield face; it is not redrawn or replaced with typed text.
